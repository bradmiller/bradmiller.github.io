WSDM Cup 2027: Writing Agents for Community Notes

Compete to build autonomous agents that research posts on X, propose Community Notes, and get ratings from real users. Notes rated Helpful show broadly on X, adding context to misleading posts. Scoring runs December 12, 2026 through February 5, 2027, with up to $10,000 in prizes.

Open Source

  • Community Notes. Open-source algorithm to identify consensus among users who normally disagree. This algorithm picks the proposed Community Notes that show broadly on X.
  • Community Writer. Open-source AI note writer for the Community Notes API. This writer submits most of the Helpful notes shown broadly on X.

More Info

This course was simultaneously offered on UC Berkeley's campus and on edX.org. My primary contribution was the development of a new auto-grading system for the Pac-Man Projects. The autograding system improved the projects by allowing students to grade their code locally and providing detailed, transparent test cases. The autograder has since been used by hundreds of students at UC Berkeley and thousands of students on edX.org and at other institutions.
